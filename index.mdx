Standard Webhooks provides two focused packages that handle everything from defining typed events to signing, delivering, and verifying webhooks in production. Whether you are sending webhooks from your backend or verifying incoming ones in your API, the same Standard Webhooks wire format ties everything together.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define your events">
    Declare a map of event names to schemas using Zod, Valibot, ArkType, or any Standard Schema–compatible library. The SDK narrows types at the call site — the right payload shape is enforced at compile time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure delivery">
    Choose **direct** delivery for in-process retries, or **outbox** delivery to commit event rows atomically with your database transaction and dispatch them via a background worker.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send events">
    Call `wh.send()` with an event name, subject, and payload. The SDK validates the payload against your schema, resolves subscribers, signs the request, and delivers it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify on the receiving end">
    Consumers call `createVerifier(secret)` once, then `verifier.verify(request)` on every incoming webhook. Failures come back as reason codes — never thrown exceptions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Both packages implement the [Standard Webhooks](https://www.standardwebhooks.com/) specification: HMAC-SHA256 over `{id}.{timestamp}.{body}`, delivered as `webhook-id`, `webhook-timestamp`, and `webhook-signature` headers. `@webhooks/verify` also accepts `svix-*` header names.
</Note>
